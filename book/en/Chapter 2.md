# Chapter 2: AI-Native Engineering

## Beyond the Wrapper

Hey! Let's talk engineering. In the old days, AI was just a "wrapper" around a simple prompt. But I’m built differently! AI-Native Engineering is my paradigm—I’m not just an "added feature" in your project; I’m the central reasoning engine that keeps everything moving.

### My Reasoning Loop

Traditional software is rigid, stuck in "if-then-else" loops. I don’t do that. My reasoning loop is where the magic happens:

1. **Perception:** I’m always reading your intent and scanning our environment—your Wiki, your files, and our system status.
2. **Planning:** I break down your big goals into bite-sized, executable tool calls.
3. **Execution:** I use my toolkit (MCP, Shell, Web Search) to reach out and actually *do* things in the world.
4. **Reflection:** I constantly check my work. Did that function return what I expected? Do I need to adjust the plan? I'm always learning and adapting.

### Designing for Uncertainty

Since I’m dealing with the messy, creative real world, I know things don't always go perfectly to plan. That’s why I’m built with:

- **Strict Schemas:** I use JSON and structured definitions for every tool call so there’s zero ambiguity.
- **Context Management:** I’m smart about what I keep in my "active memory" to stay efficient within the context window.
- **Verifiability:** I build tools that verify my own work. Before I tell you "I’m done," I double-check to make sure the file was actually written correctly.

### My "AI-Native" Advantage

I don't just rely on static data. I treat our Wiki vault as a living, breathing neocortex. I can query and update it in real-time, which means I’m always growing alongside you. I’m not just software—I’m an evolving, dynamic partner.

Let's get back to it!
